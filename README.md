# ZL2 Defelnertz - Zalith Launcher 2 Control Layout

### ⚠️ Important DPI Note:
This control layout is specifically designed for a **DPI of 600–612**. If your device uses a lower DPI, the text on the buttons may appear too large.

**How to fix it:**

To fix this, go to `Editor Menu` > `Control Appearance List` and adjust the `Text Size` under any of the available options in to your preference.

### ⚠️ Natrrator Triggerred
Pressing The Hitbox F3 + B to toggle hitboxes (Hx) while holding Left Ctrl to sprint (∆) causes the Narrator to activate.

---
### 📥 How to Install:
1. Download the `.json` file from the assets below.
2. Import the layout into your Zalith Launcher 2.
3. Enjoy your game!
